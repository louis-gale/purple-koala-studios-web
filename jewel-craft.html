Jewel Craft 3D

A game by Purple Koala Studios · Coming to Android, iPhone and iPad

A rainbow of gems. A very cute secret. Turn a colourful 3D puzzle, move connected gems onto matching colours, and reveal the charming collectible beneath.

Relax solo. Race a friend.

Take your time with offline solo puzzles, discover new workshops and admire your collection. Or invite a friend to a head-to-head online Challenge on the same puzzle.

Google Play Games and Game Center

Online Challenges use Google Play Games sign-in on Android and Game Center on Apple devices to verify your game identity. Your platform display name and optional avatar help invited opponents recognise you. Sign-in does not access your email, contacts or files. Solo puzzles do not need platform sign-in.

Your choices

The game includes advertising; optional rewarded ads can replenish Hint, Undo and Auto items. Gameplay analytics is optional and separate from advertising choices. Read our privacy policy for the data used, providers, retention and deletion instructions.
